Ladder Jump: Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · App: Ladder Jump (com.ladderjump.game) for Android

In short

Ladder Jump has no accounts, and we do not collect your name, email or location. Your scores and settings stay on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information for that purpose, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Ladder Jump is published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} ("we", "us"). You can contact us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not receive it and it is never uploaded to us:

  • Your settings: music on/off, sound effects on/off, and your chosen avatar.
  • Your top 10 high scores, each with the date and the avatar used.
  • Gameplay statistics, such as games played, ladders crossed, perfect landings, best streak, and your daily goal progress.

This data is not linked to your identity. You can erase the high scores with Settings → Reset high scores, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

3. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for this game and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The game does not use your microphone or camera.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ladder Jump is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show three kinds of ads: banner ads in the menus, interstitial (full-screen) ads between some games, and optional rewarded ads that let you revive once in a run.

When an ad is requested, Google and its partners may automatically collect and use information from your device, which can include:

DataWhy
Advertising ID and similar device or app identifiersServing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, and preventing fraud
IP address and approximate location derived from itDelivering ads and complying with regional rules
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app name and version, network type)Delivering ads that display properly, security and fraud prevention
Ad interaction data (ads shown, viewed or tapped)Measuring ad performance and paying for ads

Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for advertising. How Google uses this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy. The game needs the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions to load ads, and uses the advertising ID permission that comes with the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The game plays fully offline, but ads are only shown when you have an internet connection.

5. Your choices about ads

  • Consent form. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the game shows a consent message (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy & ad choices whenever that button is shown.
  • Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). Non-personalized ads may still appear.
  • Google ad settings. You can manage your Google ad preferences at adssettings.google.com.

6. How we use information

The data stored on your device is used only to run the game: to remember your settings, show your scores and track your daily goals. We do not use it for any other purpose, we do not sell it, and we do not share it, because we never receive it.

7. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google, through the ad SDK described above. Google may process data in countries other than your own, under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy.

8. Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you reset the scores, clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Because we hold no data about you on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete. For data Google holds for advertising, use the controls in section 5 or contact Google as described in its privacy policy.

9. Children's privacy

Ladder Jump is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help to resolve it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, the CCPA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, we generally have nothing to provide, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question. Rights relating to Google's advertising data can be exercised through Google.

11. Security

Your game data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which is protected by Android. No method of storage or electronic transmission is perfectly secure, but we take care to collect as little as possible.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the game or the rules change. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will also be described in the app's store listing or in an app update.
